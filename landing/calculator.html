WHY COSTING MATTERS

A popular product can still be underpriced.

Ingredient cost is only one part of the picture. Packaging, labor and operating costs can change what looks profitable into work that barely pays.

01

Ingredients

Use the cost of the quantity consumed by the recipe, not the full package price unless the whole package is used.

02

Packaging

Boards, boxes, ribbons, labels and presentation materials are part of the cost of delivering the order.

03

Labor

Your time has a cost. Include preparation, baking, decorating, packing and other work you want reflected in the estimate.

04

Overhead

Use the other-cost field for expenses you want included in this estimate, such as utilities, fees or allocated overhead.

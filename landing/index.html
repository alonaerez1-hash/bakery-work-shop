QUESTIONS

Frequently asked.

Is Bakery OS only for professional bakeries?+

No. It is designed especially for small baking businesses, including home bakers, custom cake businesses, dessert businesses and growing bakeries.

Do I need accounting experience?+

No. Bakery OS is designed to make everyday business information easier to understand and manage without turning your workflow into an accounting project.

Can I use it if I manage orders through WhatsApp or Instagram?+

Yes. It is especially useful when your business has started to outgrow managing everything through messages, notes and memory.

Do I need to enter all of my recipes before I can start?+

No. Start with your most-used products and upcoming orders, then build your workspace gradually.

Is this another spreadsheet template?+

No. Bakery OS is a software workspace designed to bring multiple parts of a baking business into one operating view.

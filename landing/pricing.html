QUESTIONS

Before you choose.

Can I start without entering my whole bakery?+

Yes. Start with the products, recipes and upcoming orders you use most, then expand the workspace as you go.

Is Bakery OS only for large bakeries?+

No. The product is designed around small baking businesses, including home bakers, custom cake businesses and growing bakeries.

Do I need accounting experience?+

No. The product is intended to keep everyday operational and profitability information easier to review without requiring you to become an accountant.

Can I try the product before paying?+

Yes. The Free plan shown on this site is designed as the starting point.
